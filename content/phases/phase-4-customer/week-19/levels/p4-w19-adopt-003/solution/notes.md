# Demo plan: Kozhikode branch

## Audience
Suresh, three loan officers and the internal auditor, Kavya.

## What they care about
The board wants to see faster approvals. Officers worry about their jobs and extra work. The auditor wants every flag explained.

## Demo plan
A 20 minutes slot. Show one housing loan file, with the reason for each flag, and the officer making the final decision. Finish with the time saved and a pilot request.
