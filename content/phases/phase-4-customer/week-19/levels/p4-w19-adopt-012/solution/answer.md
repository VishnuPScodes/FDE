# Set the baseline and measurement plan

## Metric
Daily typing time for order entry, and the share of orders returned for wrong items.

## Baseline
Before launch: about 3 hours a day of typing, and 4% of orders returned for wrong items, measured over the last four weeks.

## How we measure
Staff log typing start and end times in a shared sheet. Returns come from the Tally returns report, pulled each Monday and compared to the same weeks last year.

## Who owns the number
The accountant, Mr. Sharma, owns both numbers and shares them with the owner.

## Review date
First review on Monday 7 April, and then on the first Monday of every month.
