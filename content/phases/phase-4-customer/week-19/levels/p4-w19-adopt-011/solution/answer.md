# Calculate the ROI for a clinic

## Inputs
6 staff, 2 hours a day each, 26 days a month, ₹300 an hour, 60% of calls removed, tool cost ₹30,000 a month.

## Calculation
Hours today: 6 × 2 × 26 = 312 hours a month. Cost today: 312 × ₹300 = ₹93,600. Hours saved at 60% = 187 hours, so money saved = ₹56,160. Net benefit = ₹56,160 − ₹30,000 = ₹26,160 a month.

## Result
The tool gives a net benefit of about ₹26,160 a month, or about ₹3.1 lakh a year.

## Assumptions
I assume the 60% reduction holds in all eight clinics. I assume the freed time is used for other work, not for cutting staff. The ₹300 an hour is a loaded cost, taken from the clinic's finance team.
