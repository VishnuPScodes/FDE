# Write the ROI one-pager for the CFO

## Summary
The loan file checker saves about ₹70,000 a month in officer time, costs ₹25,000 a month to run, and pays back its setup cost in 4 months.

## The numbers
400 files × 30 minutes saved = 200 hours a month. At ₹350 an hour, that is ₹70,000. Less the running cost of ₹25,000, the net benefit is ₹45,000 a month. Setup is a one-time ₹1,80,000.

## Payback
₹1,80,000 ÷ ₹45,000 = 4 months. After that the bank keeps ₹45,000 a month, or ₹5.4 lakh a year.

## Caveats
The 30 minute saving is measured in the pilot at one branch and may differ elsewhere. If the saving is only 20 minutes, the net benefit falls to about ₹21,700 and payback becomes 8 to 9 months. The freed time is used for faster approvals, not for fewer staff.

## Recommendation
I recommend approving the rollout to four more branches, and reviewing the measured saving after 60 days.
