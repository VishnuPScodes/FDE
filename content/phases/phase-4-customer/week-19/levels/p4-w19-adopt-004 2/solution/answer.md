# Write the adoption plan

## Champions
The two Thrissur officers who piloted it will be champions. Each visits one new branch in the first fortnight and shows how they use it on their own files.

## Training
One 30 minute hands-on session per branch, using that branch's own anonymised files. A one page guide and a Malayalam video of three minutes follow.

## Fears and objections
Officers may fear for their jobs, so we say the tool removes checking, not decisions, and no roles are changing. Some will not trust the flags, so every flag shows its reason and the officer can overrule it.

## First 30 days
A short call with each branch every week, and a WhatsApp group for questions answered within a day. We visit each branch in week two. Problems are logged and the top three are fixed each week.

## Success signal
At least 80% of new files go through the tool by day 30, and average checking time stays under 20 minutes.
