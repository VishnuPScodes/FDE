# Announce the change to staff

## What is changing
From Monday 2 March, the scan checking tool will flag pages that look missing in a loan file, so you do not have to check every page by eye.

## What is not changing
The general manager has confirmed that no jobs will be cut. You still decide whether a flagged page really is missing, and you can ignore a flag. The tool only suggests.

## When
Training is on Thursday 26 February at 3 pm in the meeting room. It takes 30 minutes. The tool starts on Monday 2 March.
