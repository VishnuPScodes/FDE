# Report 90-day results

## Headline
In 90 days the tool has saved an estimated ₹1,80,000 of staff time at the Thrissur branch.

## Before and after
Before: 45 minutes to check a file. Now: 15 minutes, measured on a sample of 60 files each month. The branch handles 480 files a month.

## What it means
Saving 30 minutes on 480 files is 240 hours a month. At ₹250 an hour that is ₹60,000 a month, and ₹1,80,000 over three months.

## What we do not know yet
The saving is an estimate: we timed a sample of 60 files a month and applied it to all 480. We do not yet know whether checking quality stays the same in busy months, and we have only measured one branch.

## Next 90 days
Check the error rate on 100 files next month. Share the results with the second branch before they start.
