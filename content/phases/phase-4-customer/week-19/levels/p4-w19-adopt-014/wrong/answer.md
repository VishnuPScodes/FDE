# Report 90-day results

## Headline
In 90 days the tool has saved ₹22,500 of staff time at the Thrissur branch.

## Before and after
Before: 45 minutes to check a file. Now: 15 minutes, measured on 60 files each month.

## What it means
Saving 30 minutes on 60 files is 30 hours a month. At ₹250 an hour that is ₹7,500 a month, and ₹22,500 over three months.

## What we do not know yet
Everything is working well, so there are no open questions. Staff are happy with the tool.

## Next 90 days
Roll the tool out to every branch next month and share the results with the board.
