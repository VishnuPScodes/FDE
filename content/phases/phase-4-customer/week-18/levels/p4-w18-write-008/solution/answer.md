# Write the bad-news memo

## What happened
The pilot missed its target. No-shows fell from 18% to 16%, and we promised 14%. We take responsibility for not checking the phone numbers before we started.

## Impact
The 2 point drop saved about ₹45,000 a month. The full target would have saved about ₹90,000, so we are about ₹45,000 a month short for the two clinics.

## Cause
About 22% of phone numbers in the booking software are wrong or missing, so those patients never received a reminder. Reminders worked well for patients with valid numbers.

## Fix
For two weeks we will verify numbers at check-in and fix the list at the front desk, with a short script. Then we will restart reminders in the same two clinics and report on Friday 2 May.

## Decision needed
Please approve a two week extension at no extra cost, by Monday 14 April.
