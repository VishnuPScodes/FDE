# Write a decision request

## Decision needed
Mr. Iyer, I need your decision by Friday 21 November on whether to extend the loan-file pilot to all 6 branches.

## Recommendation
I recommend we extend now. The pilot branch cut file checking from 45 minutes to 20 minutes, so six branches checking about 140 files each would save about 350 hours a month (6 × 140 × 25 minutes).

## Options
Option A: extend to all branches in December for ₹1,20,000 a month. Option B: add two more branches in December for an extra ₹40,000 a month and decide on the rest in February.

## Risk if we wait
If we wait past Friday, the December training slot is lost and the next one is in February, which delays the savings by two months.
