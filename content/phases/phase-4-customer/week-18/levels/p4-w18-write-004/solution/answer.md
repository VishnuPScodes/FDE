# Write measurable goals and non-goals

## Goals
Cut stock-outs at the three pilot shops from 9% of daily orders to 4% within 90 days. Give each shop manager a reorder list every morning by 9 am. Keep the pilot cost under ₹40,000 a month.

## Non-goals
We will not place purchase orders automatically; the shop manager still approves every order. We will not cover the other 11 shops during the pilot. We will not change the billing software.

## How we will measure
We count stock-outs from the billing log every week and compare them with the baseline of 9%, taken in the two weeks before the pilot. Shop managers check the accuracy of the list each Friday.
