# Write a recommendation memo for the CEO

## Recommendation
Approve a 4 week pilot of WhatsApp reminders in two clinics, starting Monday 3 March.

## Why
No-shows are 18% and cost about ₹4.5 lakh a month. Cutting them by 4 points would recover about ₹1 lakh a month. Reminders are cheap, and patients already use WhatsApp.

## Cost and timeline
Total cost is ₹60,000 for four weeks. Results will be shared on Friday 4 April.

## Risks
Patients may not have given consent for messages, so we will ask at booking. Some numbers are wrong, so we will verify them. Results may vary by clinic, so we compare with two clinics that do not get reminders.

## Decision needed
Please reply yes or no by Friday 28 February so we can start on 3 March.
