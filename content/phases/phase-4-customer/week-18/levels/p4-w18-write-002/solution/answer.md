# Fix a design doc: non-goals and alternatives

## Non-goals
This phase will not do billing or GST returns. It will not read voice notes or handwritten photos. It will not change prices or approve credit. It only turns typed WhatsApp orders into draft entries that a staff member checks.

## Alternatives considered
Asking shops to use an ordering app would be tidy, but most shopkeepers prefer WhatsApp and adoption would be slow. Hiring a third data entry person costs about ₹18,000 a month and does not fix wrong items. Reading orders with a model, with staff review, keeps the shops unchanged and cuts typing.
