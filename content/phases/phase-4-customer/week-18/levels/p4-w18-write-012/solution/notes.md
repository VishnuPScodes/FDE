# Scope options: Kochi hospital

## Option A
Keep the 5 December board date. Deliver discharge summaries only, with doctor review on every summary.

## Option B
Add insurance claims, which moves the date to the end of January, because claims need the finance team and extra testing.

## Recommendation
We recommend Option A. The board mainly needs to see time saved for doctors, and claims can follow once discharge summaries are trusted.
