# Data and Constraints for a clinic chain

## Data
Booking records live in desktop software on eight separate PCs, so there is no single list. I assume each record has a name, phone number, doctor and time slot, and that about one in ten phone numbers is missing or wrong. WhatsApp chats hold extra bookings that are never entered.

## Constraints
Patient data is personal and health related, so we need consent before sending messages and must follow the DPDP Act. Front desk staff are busy and speak Telugu, Hindi and English. The budget is small, about fifty thousand rupees a month, and there is no IT person in the clinics.
