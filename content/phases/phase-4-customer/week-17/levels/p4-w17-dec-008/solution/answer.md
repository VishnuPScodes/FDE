# Repair a weak decomposition

## Problems found
The Goal has no number or date, so nobody can say if it worked. The MVP is the whole product, not a test. The Risk says only "data quality" and gives no fix. The Rollout launches everywhere with no pilot and no deciding number.

## Better Goal
Cut late fee payments from 18% to 8% of the 2,000 students by 31 March, measured on monthly collections.

## Better MVP
A WhatsApp reminder three days before the due date, sent to parents of one branch for one month, with a reply link to the fee desk.

## Better Rollout
Pilot at one branch for four weeks. If late payments there fall below 12%, extend to the other seven branches.
