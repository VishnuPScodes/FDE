# Repair a weak decomposition

## Problems found
The Goal has no number, so we cannot tell if it worked. The Risk says "data quality" but gives no fix. The plan also does not say who the users are.

## Better Goal
Cut late fee payments from 18% to 10% of students by the end of the year.

## Better MVP
A simpler app with fee reminders and online payments, built over three months by a small team and given to every branch.

## Better Rollout
Launch the app to all eight branches together at the start of the new term, with a training session for each branch office.
