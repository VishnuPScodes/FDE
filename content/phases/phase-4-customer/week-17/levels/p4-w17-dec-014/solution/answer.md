# From an app request to a small MVP

## Customer said
Build us an app for our drivers.

## Real problem
About 6% of daily deliveries are disputed because customers do not know the delivery time and drivers forget to mark undelivered packets. Customers complain about random times.

## MVP
Each evening, send customers on two routes a WhatsApp message with a delivery window. Drivers reply "done" or "skip" with the house number to a shared number, and replies are logged to a sheet. Run it for two weeks and compare disputes on those two routes.

## Not yet
No driver app, no payments and no route optimisation yet. We first need to learn whether a simple message and a reply record cut disputes before building anything bigger.
