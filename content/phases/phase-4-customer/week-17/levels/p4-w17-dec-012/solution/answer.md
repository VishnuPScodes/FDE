# List and test your assumptions

## Assumptions
- I assume prices are set today by the secretary from experience, not from a written formula.
- I assume past sale prices for at least two years exist in the accounts software.
- I assume pure zari sarees and cotton-silk blends need separate pricing.
- I assume weavers will accept a suggested price only if the secretary can override it.

## To confirm
Ask the secretary how a price is set for a new saree and who can overrule it. Ask the accountant to confirm whether two years of sales can be exported. Ask two senior weavers which saree types differ most in price. Confirm with the committee that a suggestion, not an automatic price, is acceptable.
