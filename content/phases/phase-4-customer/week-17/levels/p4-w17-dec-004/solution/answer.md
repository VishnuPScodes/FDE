# Risks and Rollout for the kirana pilot

## Risks
The model may read a quantity wrongly, so staff review every draft and we count the errors. Shops may write in Hindi mixed with English product names, so we test with real messages first. Orders contain shop names and phone numbers, so we keep them in one private sheet with limited access. Staff may feel the tool will replace them, so we say clearly that it removes typing, not jobs.

## Rollout
Weeks 1 and 2: ten shops, one staff member reviewing. Success means typing time falls from 3 hours to under 2 hours a day and wrong items stay below 2%. If we hit both, we add 30 more shops in week 3. If not, we fix the top error type and run another week.
