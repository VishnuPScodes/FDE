# Problem statement: export refund matching

## Problem statement
Lakshmi Home Textiles spends about 50 staff hours every month matching shipping bills to export invoices by hand. Errors in this matching delay GST refunds; one mistake last quarter held up ₹14 lakh for two months and squeezed cash flow.

## Numbers
- 180 export invoices a month.
- 50 hours a month × ₹400 an hour = ₹20,000 a month, about ₹2,40,000 a year in staff time.
- ₹14 lakh refund delayed for two months by one error.

## Assumptions
₹400 an hour is a fully loaded cost estimate from HR, not payroll data. We assume one serious error a quarter, based on Meena's memory of last quarter.
