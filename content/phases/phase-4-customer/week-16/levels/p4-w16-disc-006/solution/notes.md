# Notes: loan counter, Pala branch

## Problem
Officers type the same member details into two screens, and typing errors send files back from head office.

## Workarounds
Members WhatsApp photos of land documents to Anitha's personal phone. She types details in later. This is a data protection risk.

## Numbers
About 25 files a day. A simple gold loan takes 40 minutes, 25 of them typing. About one file in ten is sent back.

## Concerns
More screens means she goes back to her notebook. Staff were not consulted last time.
