# Shadowing notes: dispatch desk

## Steps observed
8:30 Shabeer opened the drivers' WhatsApp group (40 drivers) and scrolled for "loaded" messages. He copied each truck number into an Excel sheet. 9:15 a customer called about a shipment; he phoned two drivers to find it. 10:00 he printed the sheet and walked it to billing.

## Workarounds
WhatsApp group as the dispatch system. Phone calls to drivers for tracking. A paper notebook when Excel froze, used twice in one morning.

## Time spent
Scrolling and copying took about 45 minutes. The customer call and driver calls took about 15 minutes. Printing and walking to billing took about 10 minutes.

## Surprises
Billing works from a printed sheet, not the Excel file. Shabeer said the morning is "always like this", so nobody sees it as a problem any more.

## My interpretation
The real bottleneck is turning WhatsApp messages into structured data. A tracking tool alone would not help unless it fits the WhatsApp habit drivers already have.
