# Notes: Rao Dental Care

## Problem
When the front desk is busy with a patient, about one call in four is never answered, and most of those patients do not call back.

## Current workflow
Patients phone the front desk to book or reschedule. When the receptionist is with a patient, the phone just rings, and most patients who get no answer never call back. The chatbot was her nephew's idea; her real goal is that no caller ever gets lost.

## Numbers
About 60 calls a day across three clinics. One in four goes unanswered. About 15 bookings a week lost at around ₹1,500 per new patient, so roughly ₹90,000 a month.

## Next step
Ask the receptionists to log missed calls for a week to check the one-in-four figure, then see whether a call-back list is enough before building a chatbot.
