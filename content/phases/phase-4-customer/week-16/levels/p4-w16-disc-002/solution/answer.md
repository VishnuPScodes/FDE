# Stakeholder map: loan document assistant

## Sponsor
The General Manager asked for this and reports the result to the board. She wants faster loan turnaround so members stop going to private gold loan shops. She fears a failed project in front of the board.

## Users
Three loan officers at the counter will use the tool all day. They want less typing and fewer files sent back by head office. They fear more screens and being blamed when the tool reads a number wrong.

## IT and security
Thomas from IT runs the core banking system. He must approve any connection to it. He worries that member documents will leave the branch or end up on a public cloud without consent.

## Blockers
The internal auditor signs off any change to loan processing. If the audit trail is weak, the auditor can stop go-live. Thomas can also block us if data handling is unclear.

## Power and interest
- General Manager: high power, high interest. Manage closely.
- Internal auditor: high power, low interest. Keep satisfied.
- Thomas (IT): high power, medium interest. Keep satisfied and meet him in week one.
- Loan officers: low power, high interest. Keep informed and involve them in testing.
