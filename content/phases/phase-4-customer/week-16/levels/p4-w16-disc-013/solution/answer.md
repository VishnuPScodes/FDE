# Cost of manual stock tracking

## Calculation
3 clerks × 2 hours × 26 days = 156 hours a month. 156 hours × ₹300 = ₹46,800 a month. Spoilage: 1.5% of ₹80 lakh = ₹1.2 lakh a month.

## Annual cost
Clerk time: ₹46,800 × 12 = ₹5,61,600 a year. Spoilage: ₹1.2 lakh × 12 = 14.4 lakh a year.

## Assumptions
The ₹300 an hour is my estimate of loaded cost, not a figure from HR. I assume the 1.5% spoilage rate is steady all year and that the ₹80 lakh stock value does not change much by season.

## Problem statement
At Hubli Cold Storage, three clerks spend about 156 hours a month, roughly ₹5.6 lakh a year, updating paper registers, and about 14.4 lakh a year of stock spoils because old lots are missed. The manager wants fewer lost lots and less clerk time on copying.
