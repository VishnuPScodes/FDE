# Rewrite the questions

## Rewrites
1. Walk me through how you keep track of your trucks during a normal day?
2. What happens between a customer order arriving and a truck leaving the yard?
3. How do your drivers share updates with the office today, and what gets in the way?
4. What goes wrong most often when an invoice is prepared, and how do you find out?

## What changed
1. The original sold GPS; the rewrite asks how tracking works now. 2. A yes or no about "slow" became a request to describe the steps. 3. The app was my idea, so I asked how drivers communicate today. 4. I stopped assuming billing is the biggest problem and asked what goes wrong, in their own words.
