# Rewrite the questions

## Rewrites
1. How much money do you think GPS tracking would save you each month?
2. How slow would you say your dispatch process is compared to other transport companies?
3. How much easier would a mobile app make life for your drivers?
4. Since billing errors are your biggest problem, how often do they happen?

## What changed
I turned each one into a how question so they cannot just say yes or no, and kept the topic the same so we still learn about GPS, dispatch, the app and billing.
