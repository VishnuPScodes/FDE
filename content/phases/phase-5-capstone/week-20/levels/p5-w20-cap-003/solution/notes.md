# Discovery notes

## Workflow
Patients call or WhatsApp Meena. Slot goes into one Google Sheet. Meena phones all ~40 patients each morning.

## Numbers
About 3 hours of calls daily. 7-8 no-shows a day (about 18%). Each session is Rs 800, so over Rs 1 lakh lost a month.

## What they tried
Vendor SMS reminders last year at Rs 2,000 a month. Patients ignored them.

## Data
Google Sheet: name, phone, slot, therapist. Meena and Dr. Anjali have edit access.

## Success in their words
"No-shows down to 3 or 4 a day and Meena gets her mornings back."
