# Discovery notes: Sharma Physiotherapy

## Who I Met

Dr. Anjali Sharma (owner) and Meena Patil (front desk) at Sharma Physiotherapy, Kothrud, Pune. Thursday 6 Nov, 40 minutes at the clinic.

## Current Workflow

Patients book by phone or WhatsApp. Meena types the name, phone, slot and therapist into one Google Sheet. Every morning at 9 she phones each patient booked for that day. If someone cancels, she tries to fill the slot from a paper waiting list.

## Pain and Numbers

About 40 reminder calls a day, which take Meena close to 3 hours. Even so, 7-8 patients a day do not come, which is about 18%. Each session is Rs 800, so the clinic loses over Rs 1 lakh a month in empty slots.

## Quotes

"Monday mornings are just phone calls." (Meena)
"SMS was useless, nobody reads SMS any more." (Dr. Anjali)

## Data and Access

The Google Sheet is owned by Dr. Anjali. She gave me view access to a copy with names replaced by IDs. She will ask patients for consent before any real WhatsApp messages go out.

## Open Questions

Do older patients use WhatsApp? Can the clinic use a WhatsApp Business number? Who fills empty slots on Sundays?
