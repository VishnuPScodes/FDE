# Interview guide

## Opening

Thank you for your time. I want to understand how things work today, not pitch anything. I will take notes, and nothing you say leaves this conversation without your permission.

## Workflow

Walk me through what happens from the moment a patient books a slot to the moment they arrive. How does the front desk know who is coming? What happens when someone cancels?

## Numbers

How many slots do you book each day? How many patients do not turn up? How long do the reminder calls take, and how much does an empty slot cost you?

## What They Tried

What have you already tried to reduce missed slots? What happened when you tried it, and why did you stop?

## Closing

Is there anything else I should have asked? Who else should I speak to, and when can we meet next week?
