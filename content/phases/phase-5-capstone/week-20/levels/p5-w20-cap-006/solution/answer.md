# Problem statement

## Problem

Sharma Physiotherapy loses many booked sessions because patients forget or skip their slot. The front desk tries to prevent this by phoning every patient each morning, which takes most of the day's first half.

## Who Feels It

Meena at the front desk, who makes the calls, and Dr. Anjali, who loses income and therapist time.

## Current Cost

About 3 hours of calls each day. 7-8 no-shows a day, about 18% of slots. At Rs 800 a session, that is over Rs 1 lakh a month.

## Success Metric

No-shows fall from about 18% to under 10% of booked slots over a four-week pilot, and morning calls drop below 1 hour a day.
