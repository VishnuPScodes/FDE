# Scope: what we will and will not build

## In Scope

A WhatsApp reminder sent the evening before each booked slot, with a Reply YES to confirm option. A one-page status view for Meena showing who confirmed and who has not. All data stays in the existing Google Sheet.

## Out of Scope

Online payments, because only two or three patients a week ask and the desk UPI code already works. A patient app with logins, because patients already use WhatsApp every day. Doctor-wise reports and any change to the booking sheet layout.

## Next Steps

After the four-week pilot we will review the numbers with Dr. Anjali together. If no-shows have fallen, we will revisit payments later and write it as a separate scope with its own estimate.
