# Scope: no-show reminders for Sharma Physiotherapy

## Problem

About 18% of booked sessions are missed, and the front desk spends 3 hours a day on reminder calls.

## Success Metric

No-shows fall from about 18% to under 10% of booked slots over a four-week pilot. Morning calls drop below 1 hour a day.

## MVP

A small service that reads the booking sheet and sends a WhatsApp reminder 24 hours and 2 hours before each slot to opt-in patients. Patients reply 1 to confirm or 2 to cancel. Replies are written back to the sheet so Meena can see them.

## Out of Scope

Online payments, exercise videos, a patient mobile app and doctor notes. These are listed as possible next steps.

## Timeline

Build 10-14 Nov. Pilot 15-28 Nov with opt-in patients. Review meeting on 29 Nov.

## Data Handling

See the agreed data rules: opt-in consent, data stays in the clinic's Google account, logs hold IDs only and are deleted after 90 days.
