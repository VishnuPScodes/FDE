# Handover session plan

## Before The Session

Book 30 minutes with Meena on Friday at 2 pm, when the desk is quiet. Print the runbook and today's appointment list. Make sure she can log in to the status page on the desk computer.

## Meena Does It

1. Open the status page and read how many reminders went out this morning and how many failed.
2. Find one patient who did not reply and call them from the printed list.
3. Change tomorrow's reminder wording in the Google Sheet and check the preview.
4. Mark a cancelled slot in the sheet and confirm no reminder goes to that patient.

I sit beside her and only answer questions. I do not touch the keyboard.

## Outage Drill

I switch the reminders off without telling her which part. She opens the status page and sees it is red. She waits five minutes and refreshes once. If it is still red, she calls every patient on tomorrow's printed list by hand, as the clinic did before, and sends me a WhatsApp message. She writes down how long the calls took.

## Ownership

The WhatsApp Business number moves to Dr. Anjali. The Google Sheet is owned by the clinic Gmail account, and Meena is an editor. The Render hosting account moves to the clinic Gmail. I remove my own access on 15 December, after one more week of quiet support.
