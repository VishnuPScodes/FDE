# Measurement plan

## Metric

No-show rate: booked slots where the patient did not come, divided by all booked slots. Second metric: minutes per day spent on reminder calls.

## Baseline

From the booking sheet, 13 Oct - 9 Nov: 1,040 booked, 187 no-shows, so 18.0%. Meena timed her calls for one week: about 3 hours a day.

## After Window

17 Nov - 14 Dec, also four weeks, starting after the Marathi templates went live.

## Method

I use the sheet's status column. A slot counts as a no-show if the status is "absent" at day end. Cancelled slots with more than 2 hours notice are not counted as booked. Meena times her calls on the same weekdays as before.

## Confounders

Diwali fell inside the baseline window, which may raise its no-show rate. A new therapist joined on 1 Dec, which may change bookings. I will report both.
