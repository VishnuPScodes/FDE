# Plan for Godavari Consumer Distributors

## Why he is doubtful

Suresh has seen a scanner project fail because the pickers were never asked, and staff went back to paper within two months.

## Plan

Run a small trial on one aisle, designed with the pickers. They use their own Android phones to photograph shelf labels while counting, and the app compares to Tally. The goal is the Sunday count falling from nine hours toward three, against a 4% value mismatch today. The pickers decide whether it stays.

## Next step

Sit with two pickers and two supervisors by next Wednesday, then start the one-aisle trial the Sunday after.
