# One-minute demo script

## Problem
A fictional Indore medicine distributor gets 300 orders a day on WhatsApp, and two clerks spend three hours typing them into a spreadsheet. Late entry means late trucks.

## Demo
On screen you will see a WhatsApp message with a messy order. I upload it, and the tool shows each line as a row with quantity, item and a confidence mark. I click the two uncertain rows, correct one, and press approve. The clean sheet downloads and matches the distributor's template.

## Result
In a practice scenario on test data, entry time fell from 3 hours to 2.2 hours a day, a drop of about 27%, with a human reviewing every uncertain row.

## Next
I would pilot with one real clerk for two weeks, add Hindi order messages, and measure mistakes against manual entry before expanding.
