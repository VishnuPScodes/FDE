# My 60-second introduction

## Present
I build small, practical tools for real businesses. For my capstone I built a WhatsApp reminder service for a physiotherapy clinic in Pune. Over a four-week pilot, no-shows fell from 18% to 9%, and the front desk now runs it without me.

## Past
I started as a backend developer at a services company, where I learned to keep APIs reliable. Then I spent a year building automation for a family business, which taught me to listen to the people doing the work.

## Why this role
I like being close to the customer, finding the real problem and shipping a small fix quickly. As a forward deployed engineer I would bring that habit of asking good questions, measuring results honestly and handing over simple notes that customers can actually use.
