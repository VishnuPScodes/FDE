# Mock: a STAR answer about customer disagreement

## Situation
During a practice project for a Coimbatore textile exporter, the owner wanted me to build an accounts chatbot. Staff were really losing time on manual invoice matching, and I thought a chatbot would not help.

## Task
I needed to explain my view politely and still keep his trust, without delaying the project.

## Action
I asked to see how matching worked today and timed it with his staff. It took 50 hours a month. I showed him that number next to what a chatbot would change, which was nothing. Then I proposed a smaller first step: an automatic matcher for shipping bills, built in two weeks, which his team could try on one month of data. I offered to build the chatbot later if the matcher worked well.

## Result
He agreed. The matcher cut matching time from 50 hours to 12 hours a month, about a 76% drop, and he never asked for the chatbot again. I learned to bring numbers to a disagreement, not opinions.
